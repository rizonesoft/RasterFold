Volumetric TIFF: `tif_inspect` refuses `ImageDepth` other than 1 with `RFC_ERR_UNSUPPORTED_COMBINATION` and the detail "tiff: a volumetric image (ImageDepth above 1)" before pixel allocation (test: a 16x16x2 tiled TIFF with slices of 20 and 200 is refused by the adapter and by a TIFF to PNG job, with no output).
2. GIF admission: the submit sniff now reads the layout a GIF decodes to, walking its extensions to the first image descriptor with bounded reads (64 extensions, 4,096 sub-blocks) and applying the adapter's own rule (rgba8 only for a GCE transparent index inside the active colour table), so reservation sizing and limit enforcement agree (test: an opaque 40x30 GIF is admitted at `max_decoded_bytes` 3,600 and refused at submit at 3,599; a transparent one is refused at 3,600).
3. TIFF terms in both admission paths: the source-proportional terms (the held source, plus 2x for a TIFF source and 4x for a TIFF target) are computed once and added in the JPEG XL/AVIF branch too. Driving the fixture exposed that a default submission had no metadata allowance at all and that three copies were too few: the allowance is now five copies of the caller's metadata, or of `min(source size, max_metadata_bytes)` when the caller gives none (measured: 16x16 TIFF with 10 MiB XMP peaks at 67.1 MB of 104.9 MB to JPEG XL and 89.2 MB of 109.1 MB to AVIF) (test: both conversions succeed by default).
4. Standalone TIFF export applies `rfc_limits_check_metadata` to the carried ICC and XMP before reserving or writing (test: a 2 KiB XMP under `max_metadata_bytes` 1024 is `RFC_ERR_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` with no output under `preserve`, and encodes under `strip`).
5. Refusal reasons: the engine gains `rfc_exec_set_detail(exec, static_text)`; when an adapter's encode or decode then fails, the job reports that text as its detail. The GIF and TIFF adapters name each refusal (palette reduction, partial alpha, differing transparent RGB, unrepresentable duration, animation, partial frame, Plain Text, multi-page, EXIF/GPS IFD, colour tags, volumetric) (test: PNG to GIF jobs report "palette reduction", "partial alpha" and "differing RGB").
6. GCE scope: a GCE governs the next graphic only; one per graphic, fields taken for the first image, so a timed animation reaches its second image descriptor and is refused as animation (test: a two-frame GIF with a GCE before each frame is `RFC_ERR_UNSUPPORTED_COMBINATION` with an "animated" detail).
7. Public ABI: `converter/include/rfc_io.h` is byte-identical to its D10 T02 §5 version (`git diff 5578212 -- converter/include/rfc_io.h` is empty); the geometry moves to the internal `rfc_sniff_geometry` (`converter/src/job_internal.h`) filled by `rfc_input_internal_sniff_bytes_geometry` and the internal `rfc_input_sniff_source_preflight`, so `rfc_sniff_result` and `rfc_imported_input` keep their layouts.
