### adversarial: needs-attention

P1 **The staged pathname can be replaced before publication or cleanup.** `converter/src/output.c:1020` and `converter/src/output.c:1027` close the staged file before `converter/src/output.c:1046` and `converter/src/output.c:1050` publish it by name. On failure, `converter/src/output.c:1102` and `converter/src/output.c:1108` can delete the replacement instead of the job-owned artifact. The Windows equivalents have the same check/use separation. Replacing a checked directory with a symlink or junction can redirect staging, publication or cleanup into another directory. Anchor operations to verified directory handles and enforce the no-link policy during resolution, rather than relying on repeated pathname checks.

### integration: needs-attention

A callback or concurrently writable file can provide a BMP during sniffing and different bytes during the bounded read; a replacement satisfying the shallow BMP length checks reaches the BMP decoder without the promised format-disagreement refusal. Sniff the completed buffer and enforce agreement before decoder dispatch.

With a standalone publisher and an `exec` cancellation callback, cancellation during flush still reaches `commit_stage` at `converter/src/output.c:848` and reports success. Recheck cancellation after flush and establish the standalone commit boundary explicitly.
