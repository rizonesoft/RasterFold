### adversarial: needs-attention

1. P1 **Destroying a slot leaves live jobs with a dangling pointer.** `converter/src/job.c:370` frees the slot without draining its jobs or retaining shared slot state. Retained completed jobs also encounter this through stale-event APIs. Give the shared slot state an ownership lifetime covering every referencing job.
