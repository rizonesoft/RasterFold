### adversarial: needs-attention

1. P1 The libwebp scratch reservations undercount actual allocations. Encoding reserves `6 * pixels + 128 KiB` at `converter/codecs/webp.c:923`, but the provider simultaneously needs a four-byte-per-pixel hash chain and at least a four-byte-per-pixel transform buffer, before references, histograms, or threading (`tools/converter-src/libwebp/src/enc/backward_references_enc.c:210`, `tools/converter-src/libwebp/src/enc/vp8l_enc.c:1249`). Decoding similarly reserves `4 * pixels + 64 KiB` at `converter/codecs/webp.c:590`, whereas its pixel buffer alone includes another 17 full-width rows (`tools/converter-src/libwebp/src/dec/vp8l_dec.c:1562`). These untracked allocations can exceed the admitted budget while budget statistics remain within it. Derive reservations from the provider’s actual allocation paths, including threaded encoding, and test actual peak allocation rather than only reservation rejection.

### integration: needs-attention

2. P1 Encoding and verification disagree on EXIF-derived orientation. `converter/codecs/webp.c:883` accepts EXIF orientation 6 when the separate orientation field is unspecified. Decoding correctly returns orientation 6, but verification normalizes the unspecified source orientation to Normal at `converter/src/metadata.c:263` and reports loss at `converter/src/metadata.c:686`. Thus an explicitly permitted input cannot pass fresh verification. Resolve effective orientation consistently from EXIF and add an encode-plus-verification regression for this case.
