## Lessons

- Cold-start horizontal strips (`S = 4`, `W = 0`) reset all 7 models at 3 interior row boundaries, costing `+0.976%` (`photo`) and `+1.130%` (`tp`); pre-feeding `W = 128` rows of uncommitted pixel context before each strip's range coder (`rf_warm_rows(h)`) cuts that overhead by ~76% (`+0.237%` on `photo`, `+0.249%` on `tp`) while delivering 12-way strip-and-channel parallelism (`3 * S`) that cuts 10-photo decode wall time from `2.16 s` to `1.04 s` (`67.5%` of lossless WebP's `1.54 s`).
- Moving `ctr` or `wt` initialization out of `cm_alloc` into `code_bit7` or `cm_model` slows `photo`/`tp` set totals by `+1.7%` to `+8.4%` (`50 ms` to `565 ms`) to save `~0.3 ms` of one-time setup, because even a predictable branch or biases in the inner per-bit/per-sample loop executes hundreds of millions of times across the corpus.
