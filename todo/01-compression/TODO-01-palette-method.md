# TODO-01 -- Palette Method for Images with Few Colours

> **Goal:** Images with at most 256 colours (checkerboards, pixel art, flat diagrams, simple screenshots) code through a new method 2 that turns pixels into palette indices with near-certain equality contexts, so they are smaller than lossless JPEG XL e7, while photographs are untouched and every version 2 file still decodes to the same pixels.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Current state (verified 2026-10-26):** The format knows method 0 (raw), method 1 (CM), method 2 (palette, `RF_METHOD_PAL = 2u` in `src/rf.h` and `src/pal.c`), and method 3 (palette with two-step neighbour pattern, fallback candidate `e`, two-colour deduction, and minimum-1 counter step, `RF_METHOD_PAL2 = 3u` in `src/rf.h` and `src/pal.c`); `rf_decode` and `rf_decode_bmp` reject any method above 3 (`method > RF_METHOD_PAL2`) as `unsupported fold: method`. Section 1 moved the shared binary range coder (`renc`, `renc_init`, `renc_shift`, `renc_bit`, `renc_flush`, `rdec`, `rdec_init`, `rdec_tail`, `rdec_bit`) from `src/cm.c` into `src/rc.h`, section 2 added `src/pal.c`, `pal_encode`/`pal_decode` in `tests/rfi_ref.py`, "Method 2, palette" in `spec/FOLD.md`, `inspect` reporting (`method: palette` and `palette_colours: <n>`), and `KAT_PAL` plus boundary and rejection cases in `tests/test_correctness.py` and `tests/test_api.py`, section 3 wired `research/bench.c` and `research/ab.py` through `rf_encode`/`rf_decode` and measured method 2 across `photo` (`30,865,211` bytes, `0.9712` of JPEG XL e7, unchanged), `tp` (`6,651,583` bytes, `0.9743` of e7, unchanged), and `synth` (`207,146` bytes, down from `214,773`), and section 4 added method 3 (`RF_METHOD_PAL2 = 3u`, keeping `method == 2` decoding unchanged while encoding few-colour images with `WW == a` and `NN == b` pattern bits, fallback third candidate `e` in `(d, c, ww)`, two-colour deduction, and minimum-1 counter step), bringing `synth` to `206,566` bytes (`0.9118` of e7, geomean `0.5883` of e7 and `1.1220` of e9), `syn_checker` to `138` bytes (`< 210` e9), and `syn_pixel` to `52` bytes (`< 53` e9 and `< 58` WebP). Design: `docs/research.md` section 4.1.

## 2. Method 2: Palette Encoder, Decoder, Oracle, Spec

> **Started:** 2026-10-26T00:06:00Z

**Job:** A user can export an image with at most 256 colours to a small `.fold` file, import it back to identical pixels (the reverse of the export), and read the method and colour count that `inspect` reports.

The method itself, in one commit with every place a format change owes. The design is `docs/research.md` section 4.1: a first-seen palette of up to 256 colours, index decisions `I == W`, `I == N`, `I == NE` under a 5-bit equality pattern of the neighbours' indices, and an 8-bit literal tree for the rest, each decision one adaptive counter with method 1's update rule. Exchange and its reverse ship together: the encoder exports a method 2 file only when the decoder of the same commit imports it back to identical pixels, and `inspect` reports the method and the colour count.
