# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss concrete behavior, code and evidence; do not attack people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing private information and deliberately disruptive behavior are unacceptable in project discussions, reviews and issue reports.

Make room for different experience levels. Explain a technical disagreement clearly, accept corrections supported by evidence, and keep discussion relevant to the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to **derick@rizonetech.com**, with enough context to assess what happened. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary. They will consider context and protect private reporting information.
